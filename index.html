Multi-tenant RAG & LLM systems

How can different teams share model infrastructure while keeping their documents separate? With Prof. Kexin Rong, I explored tenant-scoped retrieval, evaluation, and a shared inference design using vLLM and LoRA adapters.

The implemented RAG pipeline compares flat and agentic retrieval on PostgreSQL QA and MuSiQue. Agentic retrieval helped on PostgreSQL, but took more time on both datasets and did not improve the paired MuSiQue result. Shared-serving performance and adapter training remained future work.
